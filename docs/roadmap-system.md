# Roadmap system

PathFinder's roadmap is deterministic first. The gap engine decides what is missing, how urgent it is, what evidence closes it, and how many hours it should take. AI may explain and organize that plan, but it cannot invent a different readiness model; the deterministic fallback always remains usable.

## Stage-aware sequencing

The 13 profile stages map to eight strategy groups: secondary school, early undergraduate, upper undergraduate, post-college transition, alternative training, graduate school, career change, and unknown. Each group defines its immediate focus, realistic experience target, application approach, competitive edge, credential window, and project scope.

This prevents common bad advice: high-school users are not sent into college-style application campaigns, early undergraduates do not prematurely prepare for professional admissions tests, career changers are not told to simply declare a new major, and graduate students are pushed to translate specialized work into evidence employers can evaluate.

## Baseline readiness and competitive advantage

Roadmaps do two jobs in order:

1. Get on track by closing real academic, skill, experience, evidence, and credential gaps.
2. Stand out by completing a concrete piece of work that others can review: solve a realistic problem, use field-relevant methods, get feedback from a practitioner or user, revise the work, and publish or present the result.

Passive consumption, generic certificates, tutorial clones, and high-volume applications without fit do not count as strong outcomes. Each substantial action should create demonstrated skill, credible evidence, access to an opportunity, or required readiness.

## Time and evidence integrity

Every gap has a concrete hour estimate, and its tactical action hours must sum to that estimate. Calendar duration is computed from the user's weekly availability. Actions include checkable evidence requirements so completion means more than clicking a checkbox.

## Credentials and changing requirements

Credential gates distinguish what is useful now, what requires upper-level standing, and what requires program completion. Program-dependent tests are verification-first: users compare official requirements for their actual target programs before scheduling preparation. Retired requirements are removed from the catalog.

Career, admissions, licensing, and recruiting rules change. PathFinder guidance is a decision aid, not a substitute for an official program, licensing board, or employer page.

## Quality coverage

The automated suite exercises every supported career at every canonical education stage: 46 × 13, or 598 combinations. It checks that plans remain actionable, hour-consistent, evidence-based, stage-appropriate, and usable without a live AI provider.
